Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. What Texfinity collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. In short: very little, nothing is sold, and your packs never leave your PC.

Who we are

Texfinity is made by Efou and Potter, based in Bulgaria. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us about anything on this page on our Discord: discord.gg/texfinity (open a ticket and say it's a privacy request).

The app

  • Your packs stay on your PC. Textures, sounds, models and settings are saved in Texfinity's folder on your computer. We never receive them.
  • No tracking. The app has no analytics, ads or telemetry. Crash notes (crash-log.txt) stay on your PC unless you choose to send us a bug report.
  • When you have an account, the app talks to our account server to log you in and check your plan (see below).
  • Discord status (optional, off by default) is shared through the Discord app on your PC, under Discord's own privacy policy.
  • Plugins you install are made by other people and can contact their own websites. We don't control what they do.

This website

  • No analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Fonts and files are served from this site itself.
  • Your basket is kept in your own browser (local storage) and is never sent to us.
  • If you log in here, one strictly necessary cookie keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It can't be read by the page's scripts and is not used for anything else, so no cookie banner is needed.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which records basic technical logs (like IP addresses) to run and protect its service, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Your account

To create an account we store your email address, a scrambled check of your password (never the password itself), your plan, and your active logins (each lasts 30 days). We email you a one-time code each time you log in, through an email delivery service that only uses your address to send it. To slow down attacks we briefly count requests per IP address and per email; these counts are kept in memory for a few minutes and never saved.

Buying Pro

You pay on Ko-fi, which handles the payment and your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card details. You then open a ticket on our Discord with your order and Ko-fi receipt. To give you Pro we keep a license record: the email it's for, your Discord username and ID, a license key and the date. We keep the Pro role on your Discord account while the license is active.

Why we use it (legal basis)

  • Your account, login codes and license: to provide what you asked for (performance of a contract).
  • Rate limits and security records: to keep accounts and the service safe (our legitimate interest).
  • Purchase records: to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

How long we keep it

Login codes last 10 minutes. Your account is kept until you ask us to delete it. License and purchase records are kept for as long as the license exists and for as long as accounting law requires afterwards.

Who else sees it

Only the services that make this work: our server host, the email delivery service, Ko-fi (payments), Discord (tickets and roles) and GitHub (this website). We never sell or share your data for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we have about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, or object to how we use it. Ask on our Discord and we'll answer within one month. You can also complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg) or the data protection authority where you live.

Children

If you are under 14, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account or buying anything.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. Important changes are also announced on our Discord.
